# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The system serves two related operating groups:

- Competition management users: system administrators, school administrators, instructors, project leaders, team members, and expert reviewers who manage competition preparation workflows.
- Teacher training users: provincial training system administrators, training cohort owners, class advisers, and participating teachers who need to finish cohort setup, account assignment, course scheduling, attendance, leave approval, reports, exports, and AI-assisted review.

Most users are not technical. Administrators need fast scanning, reliable batch operations, and clear destructive-action warnings. Participating teachers often use mobile browsers and need obvious next steps, low typing burden, and stable upload/sign-in flows.

## Product Purpose

This is an authenticated school operations platform for competition preparation and provincial teacher training. It should make routine management work reliable, auditable, and easy to complete without support calls.

For the teacher training surface, success means:

- Managers can immediately see what needs action for the current cohort.
- Teachers can complete first-login profile setup, arrival reporting, course sign-in, leave requests, and report submission without confusion.
- Bulk import, account generation, deletion, export, and AI review flows are explicit about consequences and preserve data integrity.

## Brand Personality

Calm, official, precise.

The interface should feel like a trustworthy education administration system: clear, restrained, and task-focused. It should not feel like a marketing page, a consumer social app, or a generic AI-generated SaaS dashboard.

## Anti-references

- Repeated identical card grids that make every action look equally important.
- Heavy blue glow, decorative gradients, glassmorphism, oversized shadows, or soft "AI dashboard" styling.
- Mixed Chinese-English labels in operational areas.
- Buried destructive actions or ambiguous wording around delete, unlink, reset password, replace file, import, or approval.
- Mobile layouts that force long scrolling before the primary task appears.

## Design Principles

1. Put the next required action first, then supporting status, then secondary navigation.
2. Prefer dense, predictable operational layouts over decorative dashboard composition.
3. Use cards only for truly separate objects or tools; use spacing, dividers, and lists for related information.
4. Reserve color for active navigation, primary actions, and semantic states.
5. Make every risky operation explicit, reversible where possible, and clear about what data or account relationship changes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, reduced motion, and mobile browser use. Avoid color-only status meaning; pair status colors with labels.
